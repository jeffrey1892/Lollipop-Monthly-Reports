# How the session works

For the YPO/HBS Week 2 Needs & Leads invitation.

**When:** Wednesday, January 20, 2027 · 12:30–1:30 PM ET
**Where:** Tata 100 (downstairs)

1. **Your need goes up on the screen.** The need you submitted is shown on a slide while you speak, so make sure it's clear and specific.
2. **We go in order.** Check the order before we start and be ready when the person before you is presenting.
3. **Come up and keep it under a minute.** State your need and what would help. Skip the backstory; a minute goes fast.
4. **If you can help, raise your hand and hold up your name tag.** Anyone with a lead, an introduction or a relevant experience to share raises their hand with their name tag visible.
5. **Take a picture.** The presenter, or a neighbor using the presenter's phone, takes a photo of the raised hands and name tags. That's all you need to do in the room.
6. **Afterwards, enter your leads in the app by Friday.** Use the photo to tick the people who raised their hands and rate each lead. The app also helps you draft a follow-up email to each one.
7. **Your summary arrives Saturday morning.** On Saturday, January 23, you'll get an email with all your leads, their contact details and ready-to-paste follow-up emails.
